**Problem:** The review page is the last screen before someone enters payment details. Most Perfect Pet sales come through comparison sites, so it's often the first time the buyer has seen the brand. Hotjar recordings showed people spending five minutes or more on the page, opening policy PDFs to work out what they were buying, and some leaving to check the main site and not coming back.

**Approach:** I did a competitor review, watched Hotjar recordings of the live page, and interviewed 11 colleagues from around the business. I only recruited internally because of the timeline, and expected it to be a weakness. It wasn't. These were the people who deal with complaints and declined claims, so they knew exactly what happens when the page makes the cover sound better than it is.

**Constraints:** One two-week sprint. Everything had to be built with MudBlazor components, and the policy wording had to stay word-for-word for FCA compliance. I couldn't change what the page said, only how it was laid out.

**Outcome:** I reworked the page hierarchy so the details people need at that point come first and the wall of policy text comes later. Four variants are now live in Amplitude, two on desktop and two on mobile, testing where the navigation and call to action sit. I'll add the numbers once the test has run long enough.
